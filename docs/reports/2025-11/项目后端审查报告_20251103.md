This refactoring is a good step towards modernizing the test suite with `pytest`. However, deleting test files without migrating their tests is a critical issue. The functionality previously tested in `test_new_generators.py`, `test_performance.py`, `test_relations.py`, `test_simple_batch.py`, and `test_validation.py` appears to be missing from the new test suite.
